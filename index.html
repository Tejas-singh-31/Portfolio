Scene 08 / 08

How I build

  1. STEP 01

    Find one sharp problem

    Endless short-video scrolling. Files you would rather not upload. Long videos nobody has time to clip.

  2. STEP 02

    Build it in the browser

    FlowRec records and PixelKit processes on your own device, so there is nothing to install and nothing to upload.

  3. STEP 03

    Ship it live

    Every project here is online on Vercel, Cloudflare Workers or GitHub Pages, so you can open it right now.

  4. STEP 04

    Make it feel good

    Motion, sound and interaction are part of the product, not paint on top of it.
